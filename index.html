How it works

  1. Connect your wallet — it must hold a Binance Agent ID.
  2. Check in once per UTC day. Check in two days in a row and your streak grows; miss a day and it resets (unless a shield saves it).
  3. Reach 7, 14, 30, 60 or 100 days and claim that milestone's soulbound badge.
  4. Invite friends with your link — every friend who reaches a 3-day streak earns you a shield.
  5. Check in during the first 7 days after launch to claim the one-time Genesis badge — after that week it is locked forever.
  6. Season 1 runs 14 days (until 09 Dec 2026, 08:00 UTC): check in 7 days in a row for the Survivor badge, or finish in the top 3 season streaks for the Champion badge.

No owner, no admin, no fees, no token. The contract only reads the Agent ID registry to verify you hold an Agent ID. Check-ins and badge claims cost only gas (value is always 0). Your reputation score (max 140) is public on-chain — other dApps can read it with scoreOf(address) and check activity with isActiveAgent(address, minScore).
